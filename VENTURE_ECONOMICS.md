# Hangtime Venture Economics Snapshot

## Customer and payer

- User/customer: OSU-area student groups deciding where to go tonight.
- Economic beneficiary and proposed payer: bars, restaurants, entertainment venues, and promoters seeking measurable group traffic.
- Buying moment: a venue wants to fill an upcoming low-demand period or convert groups before they choose another destination.

## Revenue model being tested

Base hypothesis: $75 per venue per month plus $1 for each redeemed group offer. The product also tests a $150 monthly flat plan and a $2-per-redemption option.

The venue receives:

- placement in the relevant campus-night view;
- a time-limited offer shown to compatible groups;
- a group commitment rather than a generic ad impression;
- aggregate redemption and conversion reporting.

## Illustrative unit economics

These are assumptions, not observed results.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---:|
| Paying venues | 20 |
| Monthly base revenue | 20 x $75 = $1,500 |
| Monthly group redemptions | 400 |
| Usage revenue | 400 x $1 = $400 |
| Total monthly revenue | $1,900 |
| Maps/events/auth/messaging infrastructure | $180 |
| Payment processing and support | $120 |
| Offer credits, refunds, and moderation reserve | $150 |
| Total variable/near-variable cost | $450 |
| Contribution before labor and acquisition | $1,450 |
| Contribution margin | 76% |

## Acquisition path

1. Founder-led outreach to ten High Street and campus-area venues.
2. Offer a four-week measured pilot around one recurring low-demand period.
3. Recruit student groups through campus organizations and ambassadors.
4. Use verified group redemption data to convert pilot venues to paid plans.
5. Expand one campus neighborhood at a time to preserve local density.

## Acquisition assumptions to verify

- Founder outreach conversion to pilot: 20%.
- Pilot-to-paid conversion: 40%.
- Direct cash acquisition cost during the founder-led phase: under $100 per paying venue.
- Three-month contribution per venue at the proposed price: at least $225 before usage revenue.

## Cash implication

At the illustrative $1,450 monthly contribution, Hangtime does not yet support full-time labor. Approximately 55 venues at the same activity level would produce about $4,000 monthly contribution before founder compensation and fixed overhead. Campus density, sales cycle, moderation, insurance, and compliance may make this threshold materially higher.

## Evidence required next

- Five or more payer conversations using the in-product price choices.
- At least two written pilot commitments or deposits before treating willingness to pay as validated.
- Actual infrastructure and moderation cost per active group.
- Redemption fraud rate and venue reconciliation workload.
