# Venture economics

All figures below are hypotheses for a campus pilot, not revenue or traction already achieved. They match the frozen payer test and the root economics snapshot.

## Business model

Students use Hangtime free. Local venues, event promoters, and late-night food partners pay to reach groups at the moment they are choosing a destination.

The in-product payer test presents three options:

- $75 per venue per month plus $1 per redeemed group offer.
- $150 per venue per month flat.
- $2 per redeemed group offer with no monthly base.

The partner receives relevant placement, a time-limited offer, a group commitment rather than a generic impression, and aggregate conversion reporting. Hangtime should never sell individual location or identity data.

## Base campus model

| Input | Base assumption |
|---|---:|
| Paying venues | 20 |
| Monthly base price | $75 |
| Total monthly group redemptions | 400 |
| Fee per redemption | $1 |
| Monthly base revenue | $1,500 |
| Monthly usage revenue | $400 |
| Variable and near-variable cost | $450 |

Modeled monthly revenue is $1,900 and contribution before labor and acquisition is $1,450, or about 76%. This does not yet support a full-time team.

The founder-led acquisition hypothesis is under $100 in direct cash cost per paying venue. At roughly $72.50 of campus-level monthly contribution per venue in the base case, cash payback would be about 1.4 months, but sales time, churn, and founder labor are not yet included.

## Scenario sensitivity

| Scenario | Paying venues | Base revenue | Usage revenue | Total revenue | Near-variable cost | Monthly contribution | Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Downside | 8 | $600 | $80 | $680 | $350 | $330 | 48.5% |
| Base | 20 | $1,500 | $400 | $1,900 | $450 | $1,450 | 76.3% |
| Upside | 55 | $4,125 | $1,375 | $5,500 | $1,100 | $4,400 | 80.0% |

The downside case exposes the main risk: low local density produces too little cash to support labor even when unit contribution remains positive. Approximately 55 venues at the modeled activity level approach $4,000 in monthly contribution before founder compensation and fixed legal, insurance, sales, or compliance cost.

## Student acquisition

Hangtime should measure cost per activated group rather than downloads. An activated group produces `plan_started`, compares destination information, votes, and produces `destination_locked`. Campus organizations, venue QR codes, ambassadors, and crew invites are candidate channels; none has a validated acquisition cost yet.

## What must be validated

1. Show the full price test to at least five venue owners, managers, or promoters.
2. Obtain at least two written pilot commitments or deposits before claiming willingness to pay.
3. Measure real redemption rate and incremental visits, not impressions.
4. Measure staff verification, moderation, refund, and support cost.
5. Calculate CAC from cash spend and founder sales time.
6. Test whether paid placement damages student trust.

A selected option in the prototype is interest evidence, not payment. A compliment or “keep me posted” is not willingness to pay.

## Expansion logic

Only expand after one campus demonstrates recurring use, credible status data, written payer commitments, and adequate contribution. The repeatable unit is a dense campus nightlife corridor—not an entire city.
