## Pilot offer

Hangtime will provide:

- A verified venue listing in the OSU pilot area.
- Up to two promoted offers during the pilot.
- A lightweight process for confirming deals, wait, and approximate crowd bands.
- An aggregate report of listing views, saves, heading-there intent, and verified offer redemptions.
- No sale or transfer of individual student location or identity data.

The venue will provide:

- One staff contact authorized to confirm offers and time-sensitive status.
- Accurate offer terms, exclusions, and operating hours.
- A simple redemption method agreed before launch.
- Feedback on value, workflow, and willingness to renew.
