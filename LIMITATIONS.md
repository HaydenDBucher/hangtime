# Hangtime Prototype Limitations

## Not production-ready

Hangtime is a course concept MVP. It should not be used to coordinate real-world meetings with strangers until identity, moderation, abuse prevention, privacy, and operational response systems have been designed and independently reviewed.

## Data limitations

- Fallback crowd counts, movement, wait times, covers, offers, network overlap, and matches are fictional or modeled.
- Ticketmaster can provide event listings when configured, but it does not validate Hangtime crowd, wait, or social signals.
- Rideshare ranges are modeled estimates, not live quotes.
- Profile photos, names, verification states, mutuals, and connected-account claims in the concept experience are fictional.
- A timestamp does not make modeled data observed data.

## Account limitations

- Without Supabase configuration, prototype accounts stay in browser storage.
- Browser-local password hashing is not a substitute for production authentication.
- Testers must not enter real or reused credentials, real social handles, or sensitive personal information.

## Trust and safety work still required

- Verified identity and age-assurance design
- Report review and appeals workflow
- Blocking enforcement across devices and accounts
- Location minimization, expiration, and consent audit
- Harassment, stalking, impersonation, and coordinated-abuse controls
- Emergency escalation and law-enforcement request policy
- Content moderation and venue verification
- Accessibility audit with assistive-technology users

## Business limitations

- Pricing and willingness-to-pay assumptions are unvalidated until payer tests occur.
- Venue conversion, redemption, acquisition cost, fraud, support load, and contribution margin are projections.
- Legal, insurance, alcohol-promotion, platform-policy, and campus-policy requirements require professional review.

## Honest demo language

Use "modeled," "fictional," "prototype," "assumed," or "to be tested" whenever applicable. Do not describe simulated matches, crowd activity, venue demand, payer interest, or unobserved usability as real traction.
