# Precommitted Decision-Rule Receipt

## Frozen rule

- Continue: at least 50% of qualified groups lock a destination within five minutes without moderator help.
- Change: 25-49% lock a destination, or at least 30% require help at the same step.
- Stop or reframe: fewer than 25% lock a destination.

## Numerator and denominator

- Numerator: qualified groups producing a `destination_locked` event during the session.
- Denominator: every consented qualified group producing a `plan_started` event, including abandonments and sessions requiring help.

## Qualification rule

A group qualifies when it contains at least two people jointly completing the task and begins without an already-agreed first destination. Course staff may approve a different minimum group definition; any change must be dated before results are viewed.
